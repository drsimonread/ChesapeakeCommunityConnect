Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Chesapeake Community Connect is an interactive map that helps people explore resilience initiatives across the Chesapeake region. It brings together projects and resources so community members can learn about the work happening in the region and discover ways to connect with it.

Sign in with Google or create an account, then select your account in the top-right corner and choose Create Post. Fill in the requested information to submit your post.

Alternatively, go to the Map tab, complete the Create a Post form, and provide your email address.

Chesapeake Community Connect was created to help people discover and share information about initiatives that support resilience in the Chesapeake region. The project was developed with assistance from SMCM's computer science students.

Use the map to browse initiatives across the Chesapeake region by zooming in and out, selecting a map pin to view more information about an initiative, and using the available filters to narrow down what’s displayed. You can also click on nearby projects to read summaries and learn how each initiative connects to the broader community.

If you have a question, need help using the website, or want to share feedback, use the Contact Us form on this page to reach the team.
