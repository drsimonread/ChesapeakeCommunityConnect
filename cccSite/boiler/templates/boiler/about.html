About Chesapeake Community Connect

Connecting research, technology, and communities to support a more resilient Chesapeake region.

Research Team

Our research team focuses on coastal community resilience. Through research, data analysis, and community engagement, the team works to understand environmental and societal challenges facing coastal regions and develop solutions that support sustainable and resilient communities.

SD, QA, and Capstone Teams

The Software Startup Simulator Capstone brings together students in Software Development (SD), Quality Assurance (QA), and project teams to collaborate on real-world software projects. The SD team focuses on designing, developing, and improving application features, while QA tests functionality and helps maintain software quality through testing and documentation. Together, these teams create solutions for real clients in a collaborative development environment.
