What it means to be a Contributor

Contributors are essential to the Chesapeake Community Connect mission. By creating an account, contributors can share stories, projects, and updates that help build resilience across the Chesapeake region. They can create forums and posts, comment on other contributors' work, and help neighbors learn from one another. Every contribution strengthens community knowledge, supports local action, and brings people together around environmental solutions and shared opportunities for change.
