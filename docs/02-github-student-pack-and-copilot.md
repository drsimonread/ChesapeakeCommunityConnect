# Applying for the GitHub Student Developer Pack (Including Copilot)

The GitHub Student Developer Pack gives verified students free access to GitHub Pro, extra Codespaces hours, JetBrains development tools, cloud credits, GitHub Copilot and dozens of other paid developer tools at no cost. This guide walks through applying. No prior technical experience is needed.

These instructions were generated using Anthropic's Claude and have not been verified. They may contain mistakes.

## Before you start

**Are you eligible?** You generally qualify if you are at least 13 years old and currently enrolled in a degree-granting programme (high school, college, university, or in some cases a coding bootcamp).

**You need a GitHub account.** If you do not have one, create it first by following [Creating a GitHub account](01-create-github-account.md).

**Use your college email address on that account.** This is the single most important detail. Verification is fastest and most reliable when your GitHub account has your school-issued email attached, since GitHub can recognise the domain automatically.

## Step 1: Add your college email to your GitHub account

Skip this step if you signed up with your college email. If you signed up with a personal address, add your school address before applying.

1. Sign in to GitHub and click your profile picture in the top-right corner.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. In the left sidebar, click **Emails**.
4. Under "Add email address", enter your college email and click **Add**.
5. Check your college inbox for a verification message from GitHub and click the link inside it.

## Step 2: Start your application

1. Go to the [GitHub Student Developer Pack page](https://education.github.com/pack).
2. Click **Get student benefits** (the button wording varies slightly).
3. Sign in with your GitHub account if you are prompted to.

## Step 3: Verify your student status

GitHub offers two ways to prove you are enrolled. Use whichever fits your situation.

**Option A: school email (fastest)**

1. Select your college email address from the list shown.
2. GitHub may verify this within minutes if your school's domain is recognised.

**Option B: proof of enrolment document**

1. Upload a photo or scan of a document showing your name, your school's name and a current date. Acceptable documents typically include a dated student ID, a class schedule, a transcript or a tuition receipt.
2. Make sure all of these details are clearly readable in the image; blurry photos and missing dates are the most common reasons applications are rejected.
3. Expect this method to take longer (often a few days), since a person reviews it.

## Step 4: Fill in the remaining details

1. Enter your school's name (start typing and select it from the dropdown).
2. Confirm your expected graduation date.
3. Briefly describe how you plan to use GitHub; a sentence about your coursework or a project is enough.
4. Submit the application.

## Step 5: Wait for approval

GitHub emails you when your application is reviewed. Approval is often immediate with a verified school email, or takes a few days if you uploaded a document. If your application is denied, GitHub's email explains why, and you can reapply with a clearer document.

## Step 6: Confirm your benefits are active

1. Once approved, return to the [Student Developer Pack page](https://education.github.com/pack) and browse the list of included offers.
2. Click through and redeem any partner offers you want; many are not activated automatically.
3. To confirm your GitHub account itself was upgraded, visit your [GitHub billing and plans settings](https://github.com/settings/billing) and look for the Pro plan.
4. To confirm your Copilot access, visit your [Copilot settings](https://github.com/settings/copilot) and check that your plan shows your student benefits rather than the standard free tier.

## A note about GitHub Copilot

Copilot access is one of the pack's most popular benefits, but GitHub has changed the specifics more than once, including pausing new signups for certain student Copilot tiers during 2026 while keeping other pack benefits (such as GitHub Pro) available. What you receive (a full Pro-equivalent plan or an enhanced free tier) can depend on exactly when you apply, so check the current terms shown on the [Student Developer Pack page](https://education.github.com/pack) during signup rather than assuming a particular plan level.

Once VS Code is set up, [guide 9](09-python-django-and-copilot-extensions.md#step-4-install-the-github-copilot-chat-extension) shows how to install the Copilot extension and to sign in to Copilot there; it recognises your student status automatically.

## Keeping your benefits

Student benefits expire and need to be renewed (typically once a year), and require that you are still enrolled. GitHub emails you when renewal is due. If your college email stops working after you graduate, make sure a personal email is also attached to your account so you do not lose access to it.
